# In-app UX audit

Audited 2026-11-08 from `src/`, `docs/user-stories.md`, `docs/features.md`, `docs/demo-script.md`, and the screenshots in `e2e/artifacts/`. Lenses: [Nielsen's 10 heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) (H1–H10) and Apple HIG. Two walkthroughs: a **first-time user** on a release build with no demo data, and a **returning job seeker** with about 10 applications. `01-pipeline-empty.png` is older than the current copy, so empty-state strings below are quoted from the code.

## What already works (keep it)

- Errors are in plain language and offer a way out: "Couldn’t connect / Check your connection and try again." (`src/lib/errors.ts:49-50`). Intake always offers "Fill in manually" (`ListingIntakeScreen.tsx:132,144,176`). This meets H9.
- Every empty state has an action, e.g. "Nothing to prep yet" → "Go to Pipeline" (`PrepHubScreen.tsx:34-37`). This follows [NN/g empty-state guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/).
- The delete confirmation names what will be lost: "…its Next Steps, prep questions, and mock interview sessions will be removed. This can’t be undone." (`ApplicationDetailScreen.tsx:97`).
- Accessibility basics are in place: icon buttons have labels ("Open settings", `PipelineScreen.tsx:76`), stage pills read "Stage: {stage}" (`StagePill.tsx:37`), banners are announced (`Banner.tsx:79`), and skeletons respect Reduce Motion (`Skeleton.tsx:30`).

## Walkthrough notes

**First run (release build).** There's no onboarding. That's acceptable because the empty state teaches by doing, which HIG [Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding) prefers ("Teach through interactivity"). But the Inbox tab (issue 3) and the Prep tab ("New" on everything) make the app look broken before any data exists. Users learn whether AI is available only when they try to add a job, not on first launch; on Android the first intake attempt hits "AI isn’t available" (`ListingIntakeScreen.tsx:59`).

**"Where does this stand, and what do I do next?"** The detail screen answers *stage* well with its vertical stage list and Next Steps under the current stage. It doesn't answer *when*: no applied date, no interview time, no "last contact". Notes can't be edited on the detail screen; "No notes yet." (`ApplicationDetailScreen.tsx:199`) has no add action, so you have to go through Edit. That's a detour for the most common post-interview action [INFERENCE].

**Accessibility beyond large text.** VoiceOver labels exist throughout (51 `accessibilityLabel` uses outside tests). Still open:
- The screen-reader pass hasn't been run (README status line).
- The Inbox tab never shows a badge, although `FloatingTabBar` supports one (`FloatingTabBar.tsx:19,131`). Email updates that change your pipeline are invisible from other tabs, for every user.
- At accessibility sizes, the detail screen's stage list breaks "Interviewing" mid-word at the screen edge (`a11y-ios-detail.png`). Whether it's clipped or wrapped can't be told from one frame [INFERENCE].

**What a real job seeker would expect but won't find:**
- Interview date and time, plus calendar sync.
- Recruiter and interviewer contacts.
- Which resume version was sent.
- Search and sort.
- Salary you can compare (salary is free text, `schema.ts:30`).
- Reminders (no `expo-notifications` in `package.json`) and follow-up nudges.

Teal tracks "Notes, contacts, and the resume you used" ([Teal](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435727-how-to-track-your-job-applications)). Huntr has a Contact Tracker and an Interview Tracker ([Huntr](https://huntr.co/product/interview-tracker)).
