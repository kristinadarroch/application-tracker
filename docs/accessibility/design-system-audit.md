# Design system accessibility audit: WCAG 2.2 A/AA (WCAG2ICT) plus Apple and Android guidance

**Verdict:** The design system does **not yet** meet WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA. The code confirms 7 success-criterion failures: 4.1.3, 3.2.2, 1.3.4, 1.4.4, 3.3.4, 3.3.2, and 1.3.1. Contrast passes in both themes, and touch targets meet WCAG, Apple, and Android minimums. Some criteria (keyboard, focus, spoken output) need a VoiceOver/TalkBack pass on a device. They are listed in [section (c)](#c-could-not-verify-without-a-device).

## Why WCAG 2.2 AA is the right bar

- HHS's Section 504 rule requires HHS funding recipients with 15 or more employees to make their mobile apps meet **WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA beginning May 11, 2027**; smaller recipients follow on May 10, 2028. Source: [45 CFR 84.84(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-A/part-84/subpart-I/section-84.84), eCFR current text read 2026-11-09. Whether a given health system is a recipient isn't checked here [INFERENCE: most US hospital systems receive Medicare Part A or Medicaid funds; Medicare Part B payment alone may not count, because federal financial assistance excludes contracts of insurance (45 CFR 84.10)].
- WCAG 2.2 AA contains every 2.1 AA criterion except 4.1.1 Parsing, which 2.2 marks "Obsolete and removed" ([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), W3C Recommendation 12 Dec 2024). Meeting 2.2 AA therefore also meets 2.1 AA.
- WCAG2ICT is the W3C's informative guide for applying WCAG to non-web software such as native apps ([WCAG2ICT](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag2ict-22/)).
