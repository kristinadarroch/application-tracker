# Application Tracker

A personal tool for tracking job applications from first listing to outcome, and for preparing for the interviews along the way.

## Language

### Applications

**Job Listing**:
A role an employer has posted, as published on a job board or careers page.
_Avoid_: Job, posting, opening, JD

**Application**:
The user's pursuit of one Job Listing, from saving it to a final outcome.
_Avoid_: Job, opportunity, lead

**Stage**:
Where an Application is in the pipeline: Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, or Withdrawn.
_Avoid_: Status, state, step

**Pipeline**:
All of the user's Applications grouped by Stage.
_Avoid_: Board, funnel, kanban

**Next Step**:
A dated action the user needs to take on an Application, such as booking an interview or submitting a take-home.
_Avoid_: Task, todo, follow-up, reminder

### Intake

**Intake**:
Bringing a Job Listing into the app, by sharing a link, pasting a link, or pasting the listing text.
_Avoid_: Import, capture, scrape

**Application Draft**:
AI-extracted Application details that the user has not yet confirmed.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, pending application, preview

### Inbox

**Inbox Message**:
An email related, or possibly related, to the user's job search.
_Avoid_: Email, mail, notification

**Triage**:
Classifying an Inbox Message, matching it to an Application, and proposing a Next Step.
_Avoid_: Scan, parse, sort

**Email Source**:
Where Inbox Messages come from; the demo uses a fixed sample set.
_Avoid_: Provider, integration, connector

### Prep

**Prep Question**:
A likely interview question generated for a specific Application.
_Avoid_: Practice question, flashcard

**Mock Interview Session**:
A turn-based practice interview for one Application, where the AI asks and the user answers.
_Avoid_: Practice session, chat, conversation

**Answer Feedback**:
The AI's assessment of one answer in a Mock Interview Session.
_Avoid_: Grade, score, review

**AI Replay**:
A development-only mode that shows previously recorded AI results, always labeled as replayed.
_Avoid_: Cache, offline mode, fake AI
